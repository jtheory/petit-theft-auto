# Characters

Names are diminutives: a deadpan nod to "petit". Nobody in the game ever remarks on them.

Look and feel echo the outlaw-couple archetype at a glance, drawn our own way. No copied faces, tattoos or outfit replicas.

## Lucita Caminito

- **Sprite:** dark curls in a high ponytail, gold hoop earrings.
- **Outfits:** orange jumpsuit (prison, release day); white tank and jean shorts (default).
- **Voice:** dry, watchful, says less than she means. Notices everything.
- **Arc:** three years inside. Wants a fresh start; doesn't fully trust that one exists.

## Jase Duvalet

- **Sprite:** brown hair, short beard, gray tank, jeans, a tattoo sleeve on one arm.
- **Voice:** earnest, a little too proud of small gestures. Calls his mother.
- **Arc:** spent three years waiting and fixing the house. Wants to prove he can show up.

## The car

- **His father's red convertible**, top down. Sun-faded paint, one primer-gray door.
- Treated as sacred by the dialogue ("You kept your father's car." / "It kept me.").

## Supporting

- **Ma** (Jase's mother): phone only. Worries about whether he's eating.
- **DJ Merle** (Smooth Seniors 101.1): dedications to the departed.
